Map view

Map the content ecosystem around a service. Starting from a page you choose, it follows the links written into each GOV.UK page and draws them as a map, so you can see how the content connects and which shared pages it sends people to.

A GOV.UK URL or path, one per line.
Use the page a user starts on, for example /make-court-claim-for-money.
Each page is one live request to GOV.UK. Higher is slower and busier for the API. Up to 300.

Try an example

  • Map the Civil Money Claims service.
    Traced from its start page, following links across GDS and HMCTS: the claim forms, the fee help, the enforcement steps.

About

A tool for looking at GOV.UK content through its own public APIs.

Three ways in:

  • Page view inspects a single page: who owns it, how stale it is, what pages hide inside it, whether there's a Welsh version, and what it links out to.
  • Estate view shows what a department publishes, broken down by content type and age, as a filterable table you can export.
  • Map view draws a filtered set of pages as a network, using the links in their body text to show how they connect and which shared pages they funnel users toward.

Where the data comes from

Everything is fetched live, read-only, from two public GOV.UK APIs:

  • Content API for the full record for a single page (Page view).
  • Search API for the search index, used for the estate breakdown and title search.

What it does not cover

  • GOV.UK only. Nothing on other government domains (service.gov.uk and the like).
  • No in-service content: the pages inside transactional services are not in these APIs.
  • Read-only. It never changes anything on GOV.UK.

Editorial owner vs policy owner

The least obvious thing the tool surfaces. The editorial owner (the page's primary_publishing_organisation) is who actually holds the pen and publishes the page. The policy owner (the associated organisations) is the department responsible for the policy it describes. They are often different: a mainstream guide may be editorially owned by the Government Digital Service while the policy belongs to HMCTS. The page itself only shows the policy owner, so the editorial owner is invisible unless you look here.
